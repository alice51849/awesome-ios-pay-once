## A practical workflow

1. Create a trip with the child and choose one safe, concrete task, such as helping check the family bag or saying thank you.

2. Keep the activity within adult supervision and the immediate travel setting. The app is not a reason to wander away from the gate or approach strangers.

3. Mark progress on the mission map together, then put the device away. Use another short mission only when it fits the family's day.

## Know the limits

- Trip Planet is a family mission game, not airline tracking, navigation, a safety system, or a promise to prevent travel stress.

- Optional family iCloud sharing needs the appropriate Apple services and connection; do not assume every sharing action works offline.

## When a simpler option is enough

A parent-made paper scavenger list works well for a single outing. Choose the app when the recurring mission map and collected trip progress are useful.

## Before you decide

### Must I buy Plus before creating the first trip?

No. The first trip and starter mission experience form the free core. Plus is an optional one-time expansion, not a paid download requirement.

### Do missions need children's photos?

A simple travel task can be an action, such as helping pack or noticing a color. There is no need to make a child photograph strangers or share personal details.
