## A practical workflow

1. An adult chooses one familiar symbol and listens with the child. Connect the sound to a word the family actually uses.

2. Open the tracing activity, watch the stroke guidance, and let the child repeat it without rushing.

3. Revisit the sound in a matching or blending activity. Use the parent area to review practice, and return to paper when that suits the child better.

## Know the limits

- Zhuyin/Bopomofo is not Latin-letter Hanyu Pinyin. This is not a complete Chinese-character reading curriculum.

- The app supports practice; it cannot guarantee first-grade readiness, pronunciation mastery, or a particular pace of learning.

## When a simpler option is enough

A school-provided Zhuyin sheet and an adult reading aloud may be enough if you only need a few symbols or prefer handwriting entirely on paper.
