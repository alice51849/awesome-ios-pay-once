A practical workflow

  1. Choose your home currency and check the rate you intend to use. A saved or manually entered rate can be used offline; fetching a new live rate needs a connection.
  2. Enter the original amount and currency, then assign a category. Add a trip or event tag when that context matters; leave ordinary daily spending unassigned.
  3. Review the category or trip subtotal against your receipts before exporting. Keep the original-currency amount and exchange-rate assumptions in mind when comparing totals.

Know the limits

  • This is manual expense tracking and currency conversion, not bank synchronization, tax preparation, or a financial recommendation.
  • Converted totals depend on the rate you select. They may differ from the final amount charged by your bank or card provider.

When a simpler option is enough

A spreadsheet may be enough if you only need a short expense list and are comfortable maintaining currencies and categories yourself.
