## A practical workflow

1. Use the calculator or an appropriate tool and check the inputs. For a split bill, confirm the total and number of people before accepting the result.

2. Give the result a meaningful name and type while the context is still fresh.

3. Find it again in history by name, day, or tool. Treat the saved calculation as a record of your inputs, not as a receipt or payment confirmation.

## Know the limits

- A saved result is only as accurate as the formula and inputs. Loan, date, tax, and other practical calculations are not professional advice.

- This evidence concerns the in-app saved-result workflow, not a measured widget-response-time test.

## When a simpler option is enough

A basic calculator and a note may be enough for an occasional split. CalDaily is relevant when searchable calculation history is part of your everyday routine.

## Before you decide

### Does every calculation consume a free use?

No. Basic calculations and history do not consume the advanced-tool allowance. Each advanced tool has three successful free results before its one-time-unlock boundary.

### Does saving a result charge or pay anyone?

No. A result records a calculation. It does not initiate a bank transfer, settle a bill, or certify a payment.
